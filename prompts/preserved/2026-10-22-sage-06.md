## What to do

1. **Survey OSM-based night and dark map styles.** Find the real colour values from each style's
   source or style JSON. Don't sample them from screenshots if the source is available.
   Candidates to check, not a fixed list. Add any good one you find, and drop any you can't get
   values for, saying why:
   - Protomaps basemaps' own dark flavour (`namedFlavor("dark")` in the same package our style is
     built from);
   - OsmAnd's night mode, and Organic Maps / MAPS.ME night. These are outdoor-oriented and draw
     forests, trails and water;
   - CARTO Dark Matter;
   - MapTiler's dark styles;
   - OpenFreeMap or OpenMapTiles dark;
   - Thunderforest's dark styles, where their values are public.
2. **For each style, a table of its night colours** for our layers: water fill, streams and rivers,
   forest or wood, grass or meadow, wetland, bare rock, ground/earth, paths and tracks, roads, and
   labels with their halo.
   - Add the luminance of each colour and its contrast against that style's own ground and forest.
   - Note any brightness cap, dimming or "night" rules the style or its docs state.
   - These figures are the evidence the checker's thresholds should be based on: what established
     night maps actually do.
3. **Licence for each.** Can its colours and style rules be copied into Forager's style, and with
   what attribution? Read it from each project's licence; don't guess.
4. **Recommend one style to copy.** Map its colours onto every Forager night layer, including the
   character layers (the tree glyph, wetland tufts, bare rock, icons) and the app's trail and road
   colours. Say plainly where it has no equivalent and what you'd use there.
   - The owner's sage-05 targets were water `#163f4b` and trees `#5d804f`, about what the app shows
     today. Show how the copied scheme compares with them.
   - The owner's priority is that the night map doesn't light up the display, and blue especially.
5. **Renders:** the recommended scheme, applied as a copy, at the same places as sage-05:
   - Ridgefield z13;
   - Crater Lake z12, with the large lake;
   - Forest Park z14;
   - Paradise z14.
   Also render one runner-up scheme at Crater Lake and Forest Park for comparison.
6. **Proposed checker rules**, derived from what the copied style does, not from WCAG:
   - a floor for areas;
   - a floor for lines;
   - a night brightness ceiling, with a specific limit for blue.
   Give each as a number with its source. Write them so they would pass the copied scheme and fail
   today's bright `#8ec5f0` water.
